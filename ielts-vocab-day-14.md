## 1. advent · n. UK /ˈædvent/ · US /ˈædvent/

**Meaning 释义**
- n. （重要事物的）出现，到来（the advent of ... 阅读/写作高频）
- n. （Advent）基督降临节（文化背景词，认知即可）

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** ad-(向) + vent(来，拉丁 venire) → 朝我们走来 → 到来；同根 event, prevent, convention

**Word Family 词族** advent *n.* | advents *pl.* | adventitious *adj.* 偶然的（进阶）

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ arrival, emergence, onset, inception, dawn / ≠ departure, disappearance, demise, end

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: The advent of artificial intelligence has reshaped the labour market in ways few predicted.
- Speaking: Since the advent of food delivery apps, I hardly ever cook on weekdays.

**Memory Hook 联想** ad(向) + vent(来) → 某物"朝我们走来" → 到来；event 也是 e(出) + vent(来)

**Collocations 高频搭配** the advent of · with the advent of · since the advent of · the advent of technology
