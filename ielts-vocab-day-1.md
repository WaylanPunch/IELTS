## 1. acquire

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **IPA** | UK /əˈkwaɪə(r)/ · US /əˈkwaɪər/ |
| **Chinese** | 获得, 习得 (技能/知识)；收购 (商业语境) |
| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁语词根 *quaerere* = "to seek"；前缀 *ac-* (ad-) = "toward" → 主动寻求并获得 |
| **Word Forms** | v. acquire · n. acquisition /ˌækwɪˈzɪʃn/ · adj. acquisitive /əˈkwɪzətɪv/ |
| **Synonyms** | obtain, gain, attain, procure · **Antonyms**: lose, forfeit, relinquish |
| **Examples** | 📖 Academic: *"Children acquire language more naturally during early childhood than at any later stage."* <br> 💬 Oral: *"I've acquired a taste for spicy food since I moved to Chengdu."* |
| **Memory Tip** | Imagine *AC* (acid) making you *QUIRE* (require) — you actively "seek toward" knowledge, just as the stomach seeks acid for digestion |
| **Collocations** | acquire knowledge / skills / a language / a habit / a company · acquire a reputation · acquisitive nature |
