## 1. adversity

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **IPA** | UK /ədˈvɜːsəti/ · US /ədˈvɜːrsəti/ |
| **Chinese** | 逆境, 不幸 (写作/口语抽象话题); 艰难困苦的处境 (阅读) |
| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁语 *adversus* = "turned against"；*ad-* (to) + *vers* (转, 如 reverse) + *-ity* (名词) → 命运转过来对着你 |
| **Word Forms** | n. adversity · adj. adverse /ˈædvɜːs/ (不利的) · adv. adversely · n. adversary (对手) |
| **Synonyms** | hardship, misfortune, ordeal, setback · **Antonyms**: prosperity, fortune, advantage, blessing |
| **Examples** | 📖 Academic: *"Research suggests that children who face moderate adversity often develop stronger coping mechanisms later in life."* <br> 💬 Oral: *"Looking back, that failed business was actually the adversity that made me tougher."* |
| **Memory Tip** | *ad-* (朝向) + *verse* (转) → 转过来对着你 = 逆境; 一串 "verse 转" 家族: adverse 不利的, adversary 对手, reverse 反转 |
| **Collocations** | overcome / face / cope with adversity · in the face of adversity · economic adversity · adversity and hardship |

- **词根**: 希腊 *aisthētikos*（凭感官感知的）← *aisthanesthai*（感知）+ -ic

- **同义 / 反义**: artistic, tasteful, elegant / unaesthetic, crude, unrefined
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *The study suggests that aesthetic preferences are shaped as much by cultural exposure as by individual taste.*
  - 💬 *I chose this flat mostly for aesthetic reasons — the light and the high ceilings.*
- **联想**: anaesthetic（麻醉）= an-（无）+ aesthetic（知觉）→ 无知觉；aesthetic 本义就是“感官能感到的美”
- **高频搭配**: aesthetic appeal/value · aesthetic judgement · aesthetic experience · aesthetically pleasing
