## 1. adverse

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **IPA** | UK /ˈædvɜːs/ · US /ædˈvɜːrs/ |
| **Chinese** | 不利的, 有害的 (影响/反应); 相反的 (方向) |
| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁语 *adversus* = "turned against"；*ad-* = "to" + *vertere* = "to turn" → 转过来对着你 = 不利的 |
| **Word Forms** | adj. adverse · adv. adversely · n. adversity (逆境) |
| **Synonyms** | unfavourable, detrimental, harmful, hostile · **Antonyms**: favourable, beneficial, advantageous |
| **Examples** | 📖 Academic: *"Prolonged exposure to traffic fumes has been linked to adverse respiratory outcomes in urban children."* <br> 💬 Oral: *"I had an adverse reaction to the shellfish, so I stuck to plain rice all evening."* |
| **Memory Tip** | *ad* (朝向) + *vers* (转) → 命运"转向"你对立面 = 不利; 写作高分替换 *bad/negative effect* → **adverse effect** |
| **Collocations** | adverse effect / impact / conditions / consequences · adversely affect · adverse reaction · in adverse circumstances |

## 1. aesthetic | UK /iːsˈθetɪk/ · US /esˈθetɪk/

- **释义**: ① 审美的，美学的 ② 有美感的，悦目的（n. 美学）
- **词根**: 希腊 *aisthētikos*（凭感官感知的）← *aisthanesthai*（感知）+ -ic
- **词族**: aesthetically（adv.）；aesthetics（n. 美学）；aesthete（n. 唯美主义者）
- **同义 / 反义**: artistic, tasteful, elegant / unaesthetic, crude, unrefined
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *The study suggests that aesthetic preferences are shaped as much by cultural exposure as by individual taste.*
  - 💬 *I chose this flat mostly for aesthetic reasons — the light and the high ceilings.*
- **联想**: anaesthetic（麻醉）= an-（无）+ aesthetic（知觉）→ 无知觉；aesthetic 本义就是“感官能感到的美”
- **高频搭配**: aesthetic appeal/value · aesthetic judgement · aesthetic experience · aesthetically pleasing

## 2. anticipate | UK /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt/ · US /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪt/

- **联想**: ante-（前，如 anterior）+ cip（拿，如 capture）→ 提前拿到信息 → 预料
