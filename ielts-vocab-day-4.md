## 1. subsidy

| Field | Content |
|---|---|

| **Memory Tip** | sub（下）+ sid（坐）→ 钱“坐”在项目底下托着它 → 补贴 |
| **Collocations** | government subsidy · housing / farm / fuel subsidy · subsidy scheme · provide / cut subsidies |
