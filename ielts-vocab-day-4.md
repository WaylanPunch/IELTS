## 1. adjacent | UK /əˈdʒeɪsnt/ · US /əˈdʒeɪsnt/

- **释义**：邻近的，毗连的（地点）；紧接的（时间）
- **词根词缀**：*ad-*（向）+ 拉丁语 *jacēre*（躺、投）+ *-ent* → 躺在旁边 → 邻近的
- **词族**：adj. adjacent · n. adjacency · adv. adjacently
- **近义 / 反义**：neighbouring, adjoining, bordering, contiguous / distant, remote, detached
- **例句**：📖 学术：*"The proposed high-speed rail line will run adjacent to the protected wetland, raising concerns about habitat fragmentation."* 💬 口语：*"My new apartment is adjacent to a night market, so I basically never cook anymore."*
- **联想记忆**：*ad*（去）+ *jac*（躺）→ 躺在你旁边；*eject*（扔出）中的 *ject* 同源，都是“投/扔”。
- **高频搭配**：be adjacent to · adjacent areas · immediately adjacent · geographically adjacent

## 2. aesthetic | UK /iːˈθetɪk/ · US /esˈθetɪk/

- **释义**：审美的，美学的；有审美情趣的；n. 美学，审美观
- **词根词缀**：希腊语 *aisthētikos*（可感知的），来自 *aisthanesthai*（感知）+ *-ic* → 与感知美有关
- **词族**：adj. aesthetic · n. aesthetics / aesthete · adv. aesthetically
- **近义 / 反义**：artistic, tasteful, elegant, visual / ugly, unsightly, distasteful
- **例句**：📖 学术：*"Urban regeneration projects increasingly prioritise aesthetic appeal alongside functional efficiency, attracting tourists and private investment."* 💬 口语：*"I chose this phone purely for its aesthetic — it looks so clean and minimal."*
- **联想记忆**：*aisth* = 感知；*anaesthesia*（麻醉）是“无感知”，反过来 *aesthetic* 就是“能感知美” → 审美的。
- **高频搭配**：aesthetic appeal · aesthetic value · aesthetic judgment · aesthetically pleasing
