## 1. abundant

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **IPA** | UK /əˈbʌndənt/ · US /əˈbʌndənt/ |
| **Chinese** | 丰富的，充裕的；大量的（环境/资源类高频） |
| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁 *abundare* = "to overflow"：*ab-* (away) + *unda* (wave) + *-ant* (adj.) → 水波漫溢而出 → 充沛 |
| **Word Forms** | adj. abundant · adv. abundantly · n. abundance /əˈbʌndəns/ · v. abound /əˈbaʊnd/ |
| **Synonyms** | plentiful, ample, copious, profuse · **Antonyms**: scarce, scant, meagre |
| **Examples** | 📖 Academic: *"Abundant rainfall supports intensive agriculture in the region but also raises the risk of seasonal flooding."* <br> 💬 Oral: *"There's abundant cheap food around campus, so sticking to a diet is tough."* |
| **Memory Tip** | *ab*（离开）+ *unda*（波浪）→ 水多得漫出容器 = 充足；对比 Day 1 *deplete*（耗尽）为反义 |
| **Collocations** | abundant evidence / resources / rainfall / supply · abundant in · in abundance · an abundance of |
