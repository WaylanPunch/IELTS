## 1. catastrophe · n. UK /kəˈtæstrəfi/ · US /kəˈtæstrəfi/

**Meaning 释义**
- n. 大灾难，灾祸（自然灾害、事故 —— 环境/社会类阅读高频）
- n. 惨败，灾难性结局（引申义）

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** cata-(向下，希腊 kata) + strophe(转，streiphein) → 剧情"急转直下"的结局 → 大灾难

**Word Family 词族** catastrophes *pl.* | catastrophic *adj.* 灾难性的 | catastrophically *adv.* | catastrophism *n.* 灾变论

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ disaster, calamity, tragedy, cataclysm, devastation / ≠ blessing, boon, godsend, triumph

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Without urgent emission cuts, rising sea levels could prove catastrophic for low-lying coastal cities.
- Speaking: Losing my passport on the first day of the trip would be a total catastrophe.

**Memory Hook 联想** cata(向下) + strophe(转) → 剧情往下急转 → 灾难；形容词 catastrophic 写作常用

**Collocations 高频搭配** natural/environmental catastrophe · avert/trigger a catastrophe · catastrophic consequences · on a catastrophic scale
