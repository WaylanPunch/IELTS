## 1. accommodate | UK /əˈkɒmədeɪt/ · US /əˈkɑːmədeɪt/

- **释义**: ① 容纳；为…提供住宿 ② 迁就，适应（需求）③ 满足
- **词根**: ac-（=ad-，to）+ commod（合适/便利，拉丁 *commodus*）+ -ate → 使合适 → 容纳、适应
- **词族**: accommodates / accommodated / accommodating（v.）；accommodation（n. 住宿）；accommodating（adj. 乐于助人的）
- **同义 / 反义**: house, lodge, hold, adapt, cater for / exclude, reject, displace
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *Universities are under growing pressure to accommodate the rising number of international students.*
  - 💬 *Our hotel room can accommodate four people if we use the sofa bed.*
- **联想**: commod 与 commodity（商品）同源——商品即“方便之物”；酒店“方便”你住下 → 容纳
- **高频搭配**: accommodate students/guests · accommodate the needs of · accommodate demand · accommodate change

## 2. accumulate | UK /əˈkjuːmjəleɪt/ · US /əˈkjuːmjəleɪt/

- **释义**: ① 积累，积聚 ② 逐渐增加
- **词根**: ac-（=ad-，to）+ cumul（堆，拉丁 *cumulus* 积云）+ -ate → 堆起来
- **词族**: accumulates / accumulated / accumulating（v.）；accumulation（n.）；accumulative（adj.）
- **同义 / 反义**: build up, amass, gather, pile up / disperse, dissipate, squander
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *Plastic waste accumulates in the oceans, where it eventually breaks down into microplastics.*
  - 💬 *I've accumulated way too many unread emails this week.*
- **联想**: cumulus 积云 → 云一层层堆起来 → accumulate 积累
- **高频搭配**: accumulate wealth/knowledge/evidence · accumulate over time · accumulated experience

## 3. adverse | UK /ˈædvɜːs/ · US /ædˈvɜːrs/

- **释义**: ① 不利的，有害的 ② 相反的，对立的
- **词根**: ad-（to）+ vers（转，拉丁 *vertere*）+ -e → 转过来对着你 → 不利
- **词族**: adversely（adv.）；adversity（n. 逆境）；adverse（adj.）
- **同义 / 反义**: unfavourable, harmful, detrimental, negative / favourable, beneficial, advantageous
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *Prolonged exposure to traffic noise has adverse effects on residents' sleep and mental health.*
  - 💬 *The weather was a bit adverse, but we still managed a great hike.*
- **联想**: vers=转（reverse 反转）→ 风向转过来对着你 → 不利；注意 averse 是“厌恶的”
- **高频搭配**: adverse effects/impact · adverse weather conditions · adverse reaction · adversely affect
