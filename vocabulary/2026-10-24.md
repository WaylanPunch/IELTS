## 1. adjacent | UK /əˈdʒeɪsnt/ · US /əˈdʒeɪsnt/

- **释义**: ① 邻近的，毗邻的 ② 紧接着的，下一个的
- **词根**: ad-（to）+ jac（躺，拉丁 *iacere*）+ -ent → 躺在旁边 → 毗邻
- **词族**: adjacency（n. 邻接）；adjacently（adv.）；adjacent（adj.）
- **同义 / 反义**: adjoining, neighbouring, bordering, contiguous / distant, remote, detached
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *The new research centre is adjacent to the university hospital, allowing close collaboration.*
  - 💬 *There's a great noodle place adjacent to my office building.*
- **联想**: jac=躺（如 jet 喷出、eject 扔出同源）→ 躺在你旁边 → 毗邻
- **高频搭配**: adjacent to · adjacent areas · immediately adjacent · geographically adjacent

## 2. aesthetic | UK /iːsˈθetɪk/ · US /esˈθetɪk/

- **释义**: ① 审美的，美学的（adj.）② 美感，审美观（n.）
- **词根**: 希腊 *aisthētikos*（感知的）← *aisthanesthai*（感知）→ 对美的感知
- **词族**: aesthetics（n. 美学）；aesthetically（adv.）；aesthete（n. 审美家）
- **同义 / 反义**: artistic, tasteful, visually pleasing / unaesthetic, ugly, crude
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *Urban designers must balance aesthetic appeal with practical function.*
  - 💬 *The phone looks great aesthetically, but the battery is awful.*
- **联想**: 记住 esthe=感觉（麻醉 anesthesia 就是“无感觉”）→ 对美的感觉 → 审美
- **高频搭配**: aesthetic appeal/value · aesthetic preferences · purely aesthetic · aesthetic quality

## 3. aggregate | n./adj. UK /ˈæɡrɪɡət/ · US /ˈæɡrɪɡət/；v. /ˈæɡrɪɡeɪt/

- **释义**: ① 总数，合计（n./adj.）② 聚集，汇总（v.）③ 骨料（建筑）
- **词根**: ad-（to）+ greg（群体，拉丁 *grex*）+ -ate → 赶到同一群 → 聚集
- **词族**: aggregates / aggregated / aggregating（v.）；aggregation（n.）；disaggregate（v. 拆分）
- **同义 / 反义**: total, combined, collective, cumulative / individual, separate, partial
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *At the aggregate level, carbon emissions have fallen, although some regions still lag behind.*
  - 💬 *In aggregate, we spent about £200 on the trip.*
- **联想**: greg=群体（congregate 聚集、gregarious 群居的）→ 汇成一群 → 合计
- **高频搭配**: in aggregate · aggregate demand · aggregate data · on aggregate

## 4. anomaly | UK /əˈnɒməli/ · US /əˈnɑːməli/

- **释义**: ① 异常事物，反常现象 ② 不规则，例外
- **词根**: an-（not）+ hom（相同，希腊 *homos*）+ -aly → 与常态不同
- **词族**: anomalies（pl.）；anomalous（adj.）；anomalously（adv.）
- **同义 / 反义**: irregularity, abnormality, deviation, outlier / norm, regularity, standard

- **联想**: an（不）+ hom（同，如 homogeneous 同质的）→ 不同寻常 → 异常
- **高频搭配**: statistical anomaly · a clear anomaly · explain the anomaly · anomalous result

## 26. incur | UK /ɪnˈkɜː(r)/ · US /ɪnˈkɜːr/

- **释义**: 招致，遭受（费用、损失、惩罚）
