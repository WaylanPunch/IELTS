## 1. abundant | UK /əˈbʌndənt/ · US /əˈbʌndənt/

- **释义**: ① 大量的，丰富的 ② 充裕的
- **词根**: ab-（away / 加强）+ und（波浪，拉丁 *unda*）+ -ant → 如波浪涌来 → 源源不断
- **词族**: abundance (n. 丰富)；abundantly (adv.)；abundant (adj.)
- **同义 / 反义**: plentiful, ample, copious / scarce, sparse, meagre
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *The region is abundant in natural resources, which has attracted heavy investment.*
  - 💬 *There's abundant evidence that sleep affects our mood.*
- **联想**: 同根 undulate（波动）——波浪滚滚而来 → 丰富
- **高频搭配**: abundant evidence · abundant resources · be abundant in · abundant supply

## 2. accelerate | UK /əkˈseləreɪt/ · US /əkˈseləreɪt/

- **释义**: ① 加速，加快 ② （车辆）提速
- **词根**: ac-（=ad-，to）+ celer（快，拉丁 *celer*）+ -ate → 使变快
- **词族**: accelerates / accelerated / accelerating（v.）；acceleration (n.)；accelerator (n. 油门)
- **同义 / 反义**: speed up, hasten, quicken / decelerate, slow down, retard
- **例句**:
  - 📖 *Rising temperatures are accelerating the melting of polar ice sheets.*
  - 💬 *The government wants to accelerate the shift to electric cars.*
- **联想**: celer = 快，同根 celerity（敏捷）→ 提速
- **高频搭配**: accelerate growth · accelerate the process · accelerate climate change · accelerate development
