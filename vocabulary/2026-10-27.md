## 1. acknowledge | UK /əkˈnɒlɪdʒ/ · US /əkˈnɑːlɪdʒ/

- **Def**: ① 承认（事实、问题）② 认可…的价值/贡献 ③ 确认收到（信件、消息）
- **Root**: ac-（= ad-，加强）+ know（知道）+ -ledge → 明确知道 → 承认
- **Family**: acknowledges / acknowledged / acknowledging (v.); acknowledgement (n. 承认；致谢); acknowledged (adj. 公认的)
- **Syn / Ant**: admit, concede, recognize, credit / deny, ignore, overlook
- **Ex**:
  - A: *The report acknowledges that current policies have failed to curb carbon emissions.*
  - S: *I have to acknowledge that I'm a bit addicted to my phone.*
- **Memory**: know 是"心里知道"，加上"说出来"就是 acknowledge；论文末尾的 acknowledgement 就是致谢
- **Colloc**: acknowledge the fact/importance · be widely acknowledged · acknowledge receipt of

## 2. assess | UK /əˈses/ · US /əˈses/

- **Def**: ① 评估（能力、风险、影响）② 估算（价值、数额）③ 对…征税
- **Root**: as-（= ad-，to）+ sess（sit 坐，拉丁 *sedere*）→ 坐在法官席旁 → 评判
- **Family**: assesses / assessed / assessing (v.); assessment (n. 评估); assessor (n. 评估员); assessable (adj.)
- **Syn / Ant**: evaluate, appraise, gauge, estimate / ignore, neglect, guess
- **Ex**:
  - A: *Researchers assessed the impact of screen time on adolescents' sleep quality.*
  - S: *How do you assess whether a film is worth watching?*
- **Memory**: 法官"坐"在你旁边 → 给你打分；assessment 就是课程里的"平时成绩"
- **Colloc**: assess the impact/risk · assess performance · a fair assessment

## 30. thrive | UK /θraɪv/ · US /θraɪv/

- **Def**: ① 兴旺，繁荣 ② 茁壮成长 ③ thrive on 乐于…，靠…过得好
- **Root**: 源自古诺斯语 *thrifask*（抓住、繁荣）→ 抓住机会 → 兴旺
- **Family**: thrives / thrived / thriving (v.); thriving (adj. 兴旺的)
- **Syn / Ant**: flourish, prosper, boom, succeed / wither, decline, struggle
- **Ex**:
  - A: *Businesses thrive in cities with reliable infrastructure and skilled labour.*
  - S: *Some plants thrive in low light, which is perfect for my flat.*
- **Memory**: thrive on = 靠…活得旺；thriving economy 繁荣的经济
- **Colloc**: thrive on · a thriving economy · thrive in
