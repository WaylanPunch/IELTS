# IELTS 核心词汇 · Day 1（2026-10-20）

> 30 个全新词条 · 词根记忆 + 词族 + 高频搭配 + 学术/口语双例句

## 1. alleviate

- **音标**: UK /əˈliːvieɪt/ | US /əˈliːvieɪt/
- **释义**: ① 减轻，缓解（痛苦、问题）② 缓和
- **词根**: ad-(to/加强) + lev-(轻，如 levitate 漂浮) + -iate(动词后缀) → 使变轻 → 缓解
- **词族**: alleviates / alleviated / alleviating；alleviation (n.)；alleviative (adj.)
- **同义/反义**: relieve, ease, mitigate / aggravate, intensify, worsen
- **例句**:
  - 学术: Governments have introduced subsidies to alleviate the financial burden on low-income households.
  - 口语: A hot bath really helps alleviate stress after a long day.
- **联想**: a + lev(=levitate 漂浮，轻) + iate → 把重量变轻 → 缓解
- **高频搭配**: alleviate poverty / alleviate pressure / alleviate symptoms / alleviate the burden

## 2. ambiguous

- **音标**: UK /ˌæmbɪˈɡjuːəs/ | US /æmˈbɪɡjuəs/
- **释义**: ① 模棱两可的，含糊不清的 ② 有歧义的
- **词根**: ambi-(both/两) + ag-/ig-(drive 驱动) + -ous(形容词) → 朝两个方向走 → 模糊
- **词族**: ambiguity (n.)；ambiguously (adv.)；unambiguous (adj.)
- **同义/反义**: vague, unclear, equivocal / clear, explicit, unambiguous
- **例句**:
  - 学术: The survey results were ambiguous, making it difficult to draw firm conclusions.
  - 口语: His answer was so ambiguous that nobody knew what he really meant.
- **联想**: ambi(=both 双) + gu → 两条路都指 → 模糊两可
- **高频搭配**: ambiguous wording / ambiguous results / an ambiguous role / deliberately ambiguous

- **词族**: comprehend (v.)；comprehension (n.)；comprehensively (adv.)；comprehensible (adj.)
- **同义/反义**: thorough, all-inclusive, exhaustive / limited, partial, narrow

- **高频搭配**: comprehensive study / comprehensive coverage / comprehensive plan / comprehensive insurance

## 30. vulnerable

- **音标**: UK /ˈvʌlnərəbl/ | US /ˈvʌlnərəbl/
- **释义**: ① 脆弱的，易受伤害的 ② 易受攻击的
- **词根**: vulner-(wound 伤口) + -able → 易受伤的
- **词族**: vulnerability (n.)；vulnerably (adv.)；invulnerable (adj. 反义)
- **同义/反义**: exposed, defenceless, at risk / protected, secure, invulnerable
- **例句**:
  - 学术: Elderly people are especially vulnerable to extreme heat.
  - 口语: I feel a bit vulnerable when I speak in public.
- **联想**: vulner(=wound 伤口) → 容易受伤 = 脆弱；反义 invulnerable
- **高频搭配**: vulnerable to / vulnerable groups / highly vulnerable / a vulnerable position
