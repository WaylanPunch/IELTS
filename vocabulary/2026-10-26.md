## 1. accelerate | UK /əkˈseləreɪt/ · US /əkˈseləreɪt/

- **Definitions**: ① 加速，加快 ② 促进（进程）③（车辆）提速
- **Root & affixes**: ac- (= ad-, 向) + celer (快速, Latin *celer*) + -ate (动词后缀) → 使变快
- **Word family**: accelerates / accelerated / accelerating (v.); acceleration (n.); accelerator (n.); accelerative (adj.)
- **Synonyms / Antonyms**: speed up, hasten, quicken, expedite / decelerate, slow down, retard
- **Examples**:
  - Academic: *Rapid urbanisation has accelerated the loss of agricultural land around major cities.*
  - Speaking: *New software helped us accelerate the whole approval process.*
- **Memory hook**: celer = 快（celerity 迅速）；"赛"跑要加速 → accelerate
- **Collocations**: accelerate growth · accelerate the process · accelerate climate change · accelerate economic recovery
