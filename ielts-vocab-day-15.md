## 1. ecosystem · n. UK /ˈiːkəʊsɪstəm/ · US /ˈiːkoʊsɪstəm/

**Meaning 释义**
- n. 生态系统（marine/terrestrial ecosystem —— 环境类阅读高频）
- n. （相互依存的）体系，整体环境（business/media ecosystem 引申义）

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** eco-(家，希腊 oikos) + system(系统) → 自然界的"大系统"；同根 ecology, ecologist, economy

**Word Family 词族** ecosystems *pl.* | ecology *n.* 生态学 | ecological *adj.* 生态的 | ecologically *adv.* | ecologist *n.* 生态学家

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ habitat, biosphere, ecological community, environment / ≠ （无直接反义）barren land, wasteland

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Coastal development has placed enormous pressure on fragile marine ecosystems.
- Speaking: I love hiking because you get to see the whole ecosystem up close.

**Memory Hook 联想** eco(家) + system(系统) → 所有生物共同生活的"大家庭系统"；ecosystem services = 生态系统服务

**Collocations 高频搭配** fragile/delicate ecosystem · marine/terrestrial ecosystem · ecosystem services · damage/threaten an ecosystem

## 2. deforestation · n. UK /ˌdiːˌfɒrɪˈsteɪʃn/ · US /ˌdiːˌfɔːrɪˈsteɪʃn/

**Meaning 释义**
- n. 毁林，滥伐森林（环境类写作必背）

**Word Family 词族** deforest *v.* 砍伐森林 | deforested *adj.* 被砍伐的 | deforestation *n.* | reforestation *n.* 重新造林 | afforestation *n.* 植树造林

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ logging, clear-cutting, forest clearance, land clearing / ≠ reforestation, afforestation, conservation

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Large-scale deforestation in the Amazon has accelerated biodiversity loss and altered regional rainfall patterns.
- Speaking: Deforestation is a big issue in my country because so much land is cleared for farming.

**Memory Hook 联想** de(去掉) + forest(森林) → 森林被去掉 → 毁林；反义 reforestation = 重新种树

**Collocations 高频搭配** large-scale/rampant deforestation · curb/reverse deforestation · deforestation rates · illegal logging and deforestation
