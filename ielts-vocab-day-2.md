## 1. advocate

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **IPA** | UK /ˈædvəkeɪt/ (v.) /ˈædvəkət/ (n.) · US /ˈædvəkeɪt/ (v.) /ˈædvəkət/ (n.) |
| **Chinese** | v. 提倡, 主张 / n. 倡导者, 拥护者; 辩护律师 (法律) |
| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁语 *advocare* = "to call to one's side"; *ad-* = "to" + *vocare* = "to call" → 把人叫到身边为你说话/辩护 |
| **Word Forms** | v. advocate · n. advocate /ˈædvəkət/ · n. advocacy /ˈædvəkəsi/ |
| **Synonyms** | support, promote, champion, endorse · **Antonyms**: oppose, condemn, denounce, discourage |
| **Examples** | 📖 Academic: *"Public health experts advocate reducing added sugar intake as a means of curbing the global obesity epidemic."* <br> 💬 Oral: *"I'm a huge advocate for napping after lunch — it's the secret to my afternoon energy!"* |
| **Memory Tip** | *Ad* (向) + *voc* (voice, 声音) + *ate* → 用自己的声音 *vocally* 支持某人/某事 → advocate; 律师 *advocate* 在法庭上 *call to your side* |
| **Collocations** | advocate for / doing something · advocate a policy / approach · human rights advocate · advocacy group |

## 2. deteriorate

| Field | Content |
|---|---|

| **Root & Affix** | 拉丁语 *deterior* = "worse"; 比较级后缀 *-or* 来自 *deter* = "from" (比较 *exterior / interior*) → 越来越差 |

| **Collocations** | deteriorate rapidly / gradually / sharply · health / situation / conditions deteriorate · rapid deterioration |
