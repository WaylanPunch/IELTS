## 1. assemble · UK /əˈsembl/ · US /əˈsembl/

**Meaning 释义**
- v. 集合，聚集（人）—— 描述人群、会议、团队
- v. 组装，装配（零件、设备）—— 科技/生产类阅读高频

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** as-(=ad- 向) + 拉丁 simul(一起、相同) → 把人和物"聚到一起"

**Word Family 词族** assembly *n.* 集会；组装 | assembler *n.* 装配工 | assembled *adj.* 组装好的 | disassemble *v.* 拆卸

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ gather, congregate, convene, put together / ≠ disperse, scatter, dismantle

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: The committee assembled a panel of experts to review the evidence on climate adaptation.
- Speaking: Every year my whole family assembles at my grandparents' house for the Spring Festival.

**Memory Hook 联想** as + semble(相似) → 相似的人聚到一起；同根词 resemble、similar、simultaneous。

**Collocations 高频搭配** assemble a team/panel · assemble evidence · assemble a machine/kit · fully assembled
