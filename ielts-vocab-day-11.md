## 1. accessible · UK /əkˈsesəbl/ · US /əkˈsesəbl/

**Meaning 释义**
- adj. 可到达的，可进入的（场所、服务）
- adj. 易获得的，可得到的（教育、信息、医疗 —— 写作高频）
- adj. 易理解的；无障碍的（残障设施）

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** ac-(=ad- 向) + cess(走) + -ible(能…的) → 能走近的 → 可进入、可获得的

**Word Family 词族** access *n./v.* 通道；使用权 | accessibility *n.* 可及性 | accessibly *adv.* | inaccessible *adj.* 无法到达的；难以理解的

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ available, reachable, obtainable, approachable / ≠ inaccessible, unavailable, remote, exclusive

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Higher education should be made accessible to students from low-income families.
- Speaking: The new library is really accessible — it's right next to the bus station.

**Memory Hook 联想** access(通道) + -ible(能) → 有通道就能进 → 可获得的；反义 inaccessible 就是"进不去"

**Collocations 高频搭配** easily/readily accessible · accessible to sb · accessible information · wheelchair-accessible

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** where + as 复合连词 → "在…之处而…" → 然而（对比）

**Word Family 词族** 无派生词；同义替换 while, whilst, although, conversely

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ while, whilst, although, conversely / ≠ similarly, likewise, equally

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Urban residents tend to have greater access to specialist healthcare, whereas those in rural areas often face long travel times.
- Speaking: I'm quite outgoing, whereas my brother is much happier staying at home.

**Memory Hook 联想** where + as → 一个地方这样、另一个地方那样 → 转折对比；写作中与 while 互换使用。

**Collocations 高频搭配** A is ..., whereas B is ... · whereas previously · whereas in the past · whereas others
