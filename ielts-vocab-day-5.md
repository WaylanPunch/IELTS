## 1. albeit · UK /ˌɔːlˈbiːɪt/ · US /ˌɔːlˈbiːɪt/

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **POS** | conj. |
| **Chinese** | 虽然；尽管；即便（引出让步或限制信息） |
| **Root & Affix** | 古英语 *all be it* = "though it be all"；*al* (all) + *be* + *it* → "即便全都如此" |
| **Word Family** | 无词形变化（连词）；同源结构 *be it*（不论是） |
| **Synonyms / Antonyms** | although, though, even though, notwithstanding · **Ant.**: because, since（非让步关系） |
| **Examples** | 📖 *The reform was broadly successful, albeit at a higher cost than initially projected.* <br> 💬 *I'm finally fluent in French — albeit only in restaurants!* |
| **Memory Tip** | 拆成 "all be it" → "就算全部这样" → 尽管。注意后接形容词/名词/介词短语，通常不接完整从句，比 although 更书面。 |
| **Collocations** | albeit reluctantly · albeit briefly · albeit slowly · albeit with difficulty · albeit limited |
