## 1. anxiety · UK /æŋˈzaɪəti/ · US /æŋˈzaɪəti/

**Meaning 释义**
- n. 焦虑，担忧（心理健康、考试压力 —— 阅读/口语高频）
- n. 渴望，热望（anxiety to do sth，较正式）
- n. 令人焦虑的事

**Root & Affixes 词根词缀** anx(＝拉丁 angere 勒紧、使窒息) + -iety(名词) → 心被勒紧 → 焦虑；同根还有 anger

**Word Family 词族** anxieties *pl.* | anxious *adj.* 焦虑的；渴望的 | anxiously *adv.* | anxiousness *n.*

**Synonyms / Antonyms 同反义** ≈ worry, unease, apprehension, stress, concern / ≠ calm, composure, serenity, reassurance

**Examples 例句**
- Academic: Rising academic pressure has been linked to increased anxiety among university students.
- Speaking: I always feel a bit of anxiety before a job interview, but it disappears once we start talking.

**Memory Hook 联想** anx 像"被掐住脖子"的紧 → 心提到嗓子眼 → 焦虑；反义 composure 镇定

**Collocations 高频搭配** suffer from anxiety · anxiety about/over sth · anxiety levels · relieve/ease anxiety · exam anxiety
